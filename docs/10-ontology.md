# The ontology

> Generated by `tools/build-phase3.py` from `tools/agent_spec.py`. Change it there.

The ontology **WindFarmOntology** names the things of the wind farm and how they are connected: 10 entity types and 12 relationship types. Notebook `11_build_ontology` creates it. Every entity type is bound to one table of the Lakehouse `WindFarmLH`; the turbine also gets its live signals from the KQL database `WindFarmEH`.

If the notebook cannot create the ontology, the tables below are the list to build it by hand: [step 6 of the Phase 3 guide](09-deployment-guide-phase3.md#step-6-ontology).

## Entity types

| Entity type | Meaning | Lakehouse table | Key | Shown as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Site | The wind farm. | `ref_site` | SiteId | SiteName |
| Turbine | One wind turbine, WT-01 to WT-12. | `ref_turbines` | TurbineId | TurbineName |
| Component | A major component of a turbine: gearbox, generator, main bearing, blades and so on. | `ref_components` | ComponentId | ComponentType |
| WorkOrder | A maintenance job on a turbine. | `ops_work_orders` | WorkOrderId | Summary |
| DowntimeEvent | One stop or derate of a turbine, with its cause and the energy it cost. | `ops_turbine_events` | EventId | EventDescription |
| AlarmType | A kind of controller alarm: its threshold, what the controller does, and the procedure. | `ref_alarm_codes` | AlarmCode | AlarmDescription |
| SparePart | A spare part held in stock. | `ref_spare_parts` | PartId | PartDescription |
| Technician | A member of the maintenance crew. | `ref_technicians` | TechnicianId | TechnicianName |
| Procedure | A standard operating procedure. | `ref_sop_index` | SopId | ProcedureTitle |
| Contract | A commercial contract: power purchase, service or warranty. | `ref_contracts` | ContractId | Counterparty |

## Rules the ontology follows

- A name is 1 to 26 letters and digits. A property name that appears on two entity types has the same type on both.
- A key is text or a whole number.
- One entity type has one Lakehouse table. Live signals come from the KQL database and need that table first.
- The Lakehouse tables have no spaces or special characters in column names. Fabric turns on Delta column mapping for such names, and the ontology cannot read tables with column mapping.
- The data of the ontology is a copy in a graph. After the tables change, refresh the graph model of the ontology.
