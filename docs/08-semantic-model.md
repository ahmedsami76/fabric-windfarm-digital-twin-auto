# The semantic model

Generated by `tools/build-phase2.py` from `tools/model_spec.py`. Notebook `06_build_semantic_model` builds the model **WindFarm Operations** from the same definition, and notebook `07_model_acceptance` tests it.

All dates and times are UTC, as in the question bank. Measures marked as site figures do not split by turbine.

## Measures

### Energy

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Energy MWh** | Energy delivered by the turbines. | `DIVIDE(SUM('Scada'[EnergyKwh]), 1000)` |
| **Possible Energy MWh** | What the turbines would have delivered in the wind they had, by the power curve at the air density of the moment. | `DIVIDE(SUM('Scada'[PossibleEnergyKwh]), 1000)` |
| **Lost Energy MWh** | Possible minus delivered energy in periods when a turbine was stopped or limited. Split it by Scada[LossCause]. | `DIVIDE(SUM('Scada'[LostEnergyKwh]), 1000)` |
| **Turbine Hours** | Hours covered by the data, summed over the turbines. | `DIVIDE(COUNTROWS('Scada'), 6)` |
| **Capacity Factor** | Energy as a share of what the turbines would deliver at rated power all the time. | `DIVIDE(SUM('Scada'[EnergyKwh]), SUMX('Turbine', 'Turbine'[RatedPowerKw] * CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Scada'))) / 6)` |
| **Average Wind Speed** | Mean wind speed at the nacelles, m/s. | `AVERAGE('Scada'[WindSpeed])` |
| **CO2 Avoided t** | Energy times the average emission intensity of the grid, in tonnes. | `DIVIDE([Energy MWh] * MAX('Site'[GridEmissionFactorKgCo2PerMwh]), 1000)` |

### Availability

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Time Availability** | Share of the time in which a turbine was not down for a fault or for maintenance. | `VAR known = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Scada'), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[AvailabilityCategory] <> "Information unavailable")) VAR down = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Scada'), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[AvailabilityCategory] IN {"Forced outage", "Scheduled maintenance", "Planned corrective action"})) RETURN DIVIDE(known - down, known)` |
| **Energy Availability** | Share of the energy that was not lost to the turbine itself. | `VAR energy = CALCULATE(SUM('Scada'[EnergyKwh]), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[AvailabilityCategory] <> "Information unavailable")) VAR lost = CALCULATE(SUM('Scada'[LostEnergyKwh]), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[AvailabilityCategory] <> "Information unavailable"), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[LossCause] IN {"Component fault", "Scheduled maintenance", "Corrective maintenance", "Technical derate"})) RETURN DIVIDE(energy, energy + lost)` |
| **Forced Outage Hours** | Hours stopped by a fault. | `DIVIDE(CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Scada'), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[AvailabilityCategory] = "Forced outage")), 6)` |

### Performance

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Performance Index** | Energy as a share of possible energy while the turbine ran without any limit. Below 1: worse than the power curve. | `CALCULATE(DIVIDE(SUM('Scada'[EnergyKwh]), SUM('Scada'[PossibleEnergyKwh])), KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[AvailabilityCategory] = "Full performance"))` |
| **Average Gearbox Bearing Temp** | degC. | `AVERAGE('Scada'[GearboxBearingTemp])` |
| **Average Gearbox Vibration** | mm/s RMS. | `AVERAGE('Scada'[GearboxVibration])` |

### Curtailment

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Curtailed Energy MWh** | Energy not produced because the grid operator or the buyer limited the site. | `CALCULATE([Lost Energy MWh], KEEPFILTERS('Scada'[LossCause] = "Grid curtailment"))` |
| **Curtailment Share** | Curtailed energy as a share of energy plus all lost energy. | `DIVIDE([Curtailed Energy MWh], [Energy MWh] + [Lost Energy MWh])` |
| **Deemed Generation MWh** | Curtailed energy that the buyer pays for. A site figure: it does not split by turbine. | `CALCULATE(SUM('Curtailments'[LostEnergyMwh]), KEEPFILTERS('Curtailments'[Compensation] = "Paid"))` |
| **Unpaid Curtailment MWh** | Curtailed energy that nobody pays for. A site figure. | `CALCULATE(SUM('Curtailments'[LostEnergyMwh]), KEEPFILTERS('Curtailments'[Compensation] = "Not paid"))` |

### Reliability

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Failures** | Faults that needed a technician. | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Events'), KEEPFILTERS('Events'[AvailabilityCategory] = "Forced outage"), KEEPFILTERS('Events'[Cause] = "Component fault"))` |
| **Failure Downtime Hours** | Hours from each fault to the return to service. | `CALCULATE(SUM('Events'[DurationHours]), KEEPFILTERS('Events'[AvailabilityCategory] = "Forced outage"), KEEPFILTERS('Events'[Cause] = "Component fault"))` |
| **MTTR Hours** | Mean time to repair, including waiting for access, parts and people. | `DIVIDE([Failure Downtime Hours], [Failures])` |
| **MTBF Hours** | Mean time between failures: running hours per failure. | `DIVIDE([Turbine Hours] - [Failure Downtime Hours], [Failures])` |
| **Downtime Events** | Stops and outages of every kind. | `COUNTROWS('Events')` |

### Maintenance

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Open Work Orders** | Jobs that are not closed: open, in progress or planned. | `CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Work Orders'), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[Status] <> "Closed"))` |
| **Repair Cost Paid by Owner USD** | Closed corrective work invoiced to the owner, by the day the job was opened. | `CALCULATE(SUM('Work Orders'[TotalCostUsd]), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[WorkType] = "Corrective"), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[Status] = "Closed"), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[CostBorneBy] = "Owner"))` |
| **Repair Cost Covered by Warranty USD** | Closed corrective work that a warranty paid. | `CALCULATE(SUM('Work Orders'[TotalCostUsd]), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[WorkType] = "Corrective"), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[Status] = "Closed"), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[CostBorneBy] = "Warranty"))` |
| **Repair Cost USD per MWh** | Owner-paid repairs per MWh produced. | `DIVIDE([Repair Cost Paid by Owner USD], [Energy MWh])` |
| **PM Compliance** | Share of the preventive work due in the period that was done within a week of its planned date. | `VAR due = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Work Orders'), KEEPFILTERS(NOT ISBLANK('Work Orders'[PmResult])), USERELATIONSHIP('Work Orders'[ScheduledDate], 'Date'[Date])) VAR in_time = CALCULATE(COUNTROWS('Work Orders'), KEEPFILTERS('Work Orders'[PmResult] = "Done within a week of the plan"), USERELATIONSHIP('Work Orders'[ScheduledDate], 'Date'[Date])) RETURN DIVIDE(in_time, due)` |

### Money

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract Price USD per MWh** | Price of the power purchase agreement. | `MAX('Site'[PpaPriceUsdPerMwh])` |
| **Revenue USD** | Energy at the contract price, plus curtailed energy that the buyer pays for. A site figure. | `([Energy MWh] + [Deemed Generation MWh]) * [Contract Price USD per MWh]` |
| **Lost Energy Value USD** | Lost energy at the contract price. | `[Lost Energy MWh] * [Contract Price USD per MWh]` |

### Budget

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Budget P50 MWh** | Budget energy, whole months. | `SUM('Budget'[P50EnergyMwh])` |
| **Budget P90 MWh** | The cautious case, whole months. | `SUM('Budget'[P90EnergyMwh])` |
| **Budget Revenue USD** | P50 energy at the contract price. | `SUM('Budget'[BudgetRevenueUsd])` |
| **Budget Repair Cost USD** | Budget for corrective maintenance. | `SUM('Budget'[BudgetCorrectiveMaintenanceUsd])` |
| **Energy vs Budget** | Energy as a share of the P50 budget. Meaningful for whole months. | `DIVIDE([Energy MWh], [Budget P50 MWh])` |

### Market

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Captured Price USD per MWh** | The real-time market price weighted by when the site produced. | `DIVIDE(SUMX('Market', 'Market'[SiteEnergyMwh] * 'Market'[RealTimePriceUsdPerMwh]), SUM('Market'[SiteEnergyMwh]))` |
| **Mean Real-Time Price USD per MWh** | Plain average of the hourly real-time price. | `AVERAGE('Market'[RealTimePriceUsdPerMwh])` |
| **Forecast Error** | Mean absolute difference between the day-ahead forecast and possible power, as a share of the site's capacity. | `DIVIDE(AVERAGE('Forecast'[AbsErrorMw]), MAX('Site'[CapacityMw]))` |

### Energy

| Measure | Meaning | DAX |
|---|---|---|
| **Wind Index** | Mean wind speed at the met mast relative to the long-term mean of the same months. Meaningful for whole months. | `DIVIDE(AVERAGE('Met'[WindSpeed]), AVERAGE('Budget'[LongTermWindSpeed]))` |
